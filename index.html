About Paws & Care

Paws & Care is a non-profit organization dedicated to rescuing, rehabilitating, and rehoming animals in need. Our team of passionate volunteers works tirelessly to provide medical care, shelter, and love to animals who have been abandoned, abused, or neglected.

Since our founding in 2015, we've rescued over 2,000 animals and found loving homes for more than 1,800 of them. We believe every animal deserves a second chance at happiness.
